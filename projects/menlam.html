LƯU CHUYỂN: COLLECTION 01

Visual Identity/ Illustration
Packaging Design

Lưu chuyển is an ongoing series of projects exploring what happens when familiar visual languages are removed from their original contexts and placed into new ones. Out of Altar/Collection 01 focuses on Vietnamese's Men Lam (Blue and white ceramics), a visual tradition closely associated with the Buddhist altar and ritual practice. Rather than treating Men lam as a decorative motif to be preserved within its traditional setting, the project takes its visual language out of the altar and into everyday consumer culture through a collection of packaging designs. The work explores how the meaning and perception of a visual language can shift when it is detached from its original context and encountered in an unfamiliar, contemporary setting.
